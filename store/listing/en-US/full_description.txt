Beatreffer trains your inner metronome. A click sets the beat and you tap along on a big button. Step by step the click fades out or disappears completely, and you have to keep the time yourself.

How it works
• Pick a tempo and a duration, then start
• Every tap is rated instantly: early, late or right on the beat
• After each session you get a summary with score and deviations

Simple mode
4/4 time, every beat, with sound, count-in only or fully silent. Perfect to get started.

Advanced mode
• Any time signature with accent groups (e.g. 7/8 as 2+2+3)
• Subdivisions and rhythm patterns
• Fixed or rotating target beat
• Six fade-out levels for the click
• Adjustable tolerance window and scoring of extra taps

Track your progress
• History with charts
• Comparison with the last 7 and 30 days
• Personal bests and weak-spot analysis
• Suggestions for the next level
• CSV export, optionally with every single tap

Precise
The click is generated sample-accurately, and your device latency can be calibrated.

No ads, no account, no internet. All data stays on your device.
